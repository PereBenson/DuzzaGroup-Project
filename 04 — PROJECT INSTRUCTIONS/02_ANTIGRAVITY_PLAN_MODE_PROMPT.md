Read:
- `00_START_HERE_DUZZA.md`

## Research comparison is required

For every important recommendation, state:
1. What the source brief says.
2. What the competitive analysis observed.
3. What the supplied visual references demonstrate.
4. What you recommend for DUZZA.
5. Whether that recommendation is a fact, an interpretation or a decision requiring owner approval.
