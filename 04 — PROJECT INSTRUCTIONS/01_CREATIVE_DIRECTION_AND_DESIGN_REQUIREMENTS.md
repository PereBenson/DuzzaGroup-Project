## Business structure

### Active businesses

**DUZZA PROJECTS**
Engineering, construction and project services; oil and gas-related work; marine and hydrographic/hydro-spatial survey support; survey-related services; hire of hydrographic navigation and positioning equipment; procurement and general supply.

Use the original briefs to establish the exact service descriptions. Do not invent technical capabilities, equipment models, project history or certifications.

**DUZZA BRANDING**
Executive branding, corporate and digital branding, creative support, graphic and media content, UGC-style content and promotional assets, including work connected to sports brands.

Present this business as a creative company. Its own website must demonstrate visual quality, not simply claim to offer it.

### Future businesses

**FASHION — COMING SOON**

**FOOD — COMING SOON**

These should be visible in the Businesses navigation and accessible through honest teaser pages. They are not active shops, restaurants or ordering services. Do not create fake products, menus, prices or launch dates.

## Navigation principles

Keep two distinct navigation concepts:

- **Businesses:** where visitors discover the companies — All Businesses, Projects, Branding, Fashion and Food.
- **Work:** what the businesses have delivered or can demonstrate. Only publish real, approved work and meaningful categories.

Create separate dropdown or mega-menu experiences for Businesses and Work. Study the supplied PSCM HTML and the GitHub menu reference for interaction ideas, but do not copy their design.

## Design principles

- Clean, spacious and confident.
- Image-led, with a full-width hero image treatment.
- Sharp-edged rectangular cards.
- Large imagery for creative work, especially Branding.
- Concise, direct copy. Avoid long paragraphs and generic corporate language.
- Purposeful motion and interaction, not decoration for its own sake.
- Mobile-first layouts designed for touch and smaller screens.
- A shared parent identity, with distinct visual expression for Projects and Branding.
- Do not assume the previous charcoal-and-lime palette is approved.
- Do not lock the previous font pairing without comparison and review.

## Contact and social

Reserve footer positions for Instagram, LinkedIn and X. Do not make missing or unconfirmed social destinations clickable.

Use a `mailto:` link only after the correct recipient address is confirmed. Until then, use a clearly labelled internal placeholder. Never invent the email address.

## Non-negotiable accuracy rules

Do not invent:
- Clients, client logos or testimonials.
- Project names, outcomes, statistics or locations.
- Equipment inventory, specifications or availability.
- Certifications, registrations or memberships.
- Leadership biographies or company history.
- Contact details, email addresses or social URLs.
- Fashion/Food launch dates or operational services.

Mark missing information clearly for the owner to supply.

## Working process

Start in Plan mode.

First inspect the project, source files, references and technical setup. Then produce an audit and a proposed implementation plan. Do not write or replace website code until the user has reviewed and approved the plan and creative direction.

The user values direct, plain English. Be focused, compare recommendations against the research, explain trade-offs and identify uncertainty. Do not disguise assumptions as facts.
